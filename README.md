# ResumeIQ 🤖

An AI-powered resume analyzer that instantly scores your resume against a job description, identifies skill gaps, and gives tailored improvement suggestions — no signup required, completely private.

🔗 **Live Demo:** https://career-enhancer-suite.lovable.app

## Features

- **ATS Score** — instant match score between your resume and a job description
- **Skill gap analysis** — identifies missing skills the employer is looking for
- **Improvement suggestions** — tailored tips to make your resume stronger
- **PDF & DOCX parsing** — upload your resume or paste the text directly
- **No signup required** — completely private, nothing is stored

## Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** React, TanStack Start
- **AI/NLP:** Semantic matching and natural language processing
- **Built with:** Lovable AI

## How to Use

1. Go to the [live app](https://career-enhancer-suite.lovable.app)
2. Upload your resume (PDF, DOCX, or TXT) or paste the text
3. Paste the job description you are applying for
4. Click **Run AI Analysis**
5. Get your ATS score, skill gaps, and improvement suggestions instantly
